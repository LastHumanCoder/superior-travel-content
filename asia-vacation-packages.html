Popular countries and combinations for first-timers

If this is your first time in the region, a few destinations consistently make for a rewarding introduction. I help you choose based on your interests, travel style and how much ground you want to cover.

Japan

A favorite first Asia trip for its safety, food, rail network and blend of ancient and modern. A classic loop links Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, easily connected by bullet train and often extended to Hakone or Hiroshima.

Thailand

Bangkok's temples and street food pair naturally with the northern culture of Chiang Mai and the beaches of Phuket or Krabi, which is why Thailand anchors so many asian vacation packages for couples and families alike.

Vietnam

A long, varied country that rewards a north-to-south route through Hanoi, Halong Bay, Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City, with strong value and a rich food scene.

India

The Golden Triangle of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur delivers the Taj Mahal and Rajasthan's palaces, and can extend to Kerala's backwaters or a tiger safari for a longer trip.

Multi-country tours

When you want to see more in one journey, I design asia vacation tour packages that combine two or three countries, such as Thailand with Vietnam and Cambodia, or Japan with a Southeast Asia extension, sequenced so the internal flights and pacing make sense.

How far in advance to plan

For most Asia vacation packages, start six to twelve months ahead. Long-haul flight availability, well-located hotels, popular guides and rail passes are limited, and peak windows such as Japan's cherry blossom and fall foliage, or Thailand's dry season, book out early. Multi-country trips need the most lead time because each internal flight, hotel and transfer has to line up. If your dates are sooner, I will tell you honestly what is still realistic and shape the trip around what is available.

Seasons and monsoons to plan around

Asia does not have one climate. Southeast Asia sees monsoon seasons that shift by region, so the rainy window in one country can be the dry, ideal window next door. Japan has a distinct rainy season in early summer and typhoon activity later in the year, while much of India travels best in the cooler months. Getting the timing right is one of the biggest reasons to plan asian vacation packages with an advisor rather than guessing, because I route your itinerary toward the better weather for each stop.
