The short answer

The best honeymoon destinations are the ones that match how you two want to feel, whether that is barefoot on a beach, out on a game drive, or wandering a European city. Advisor Lynne Kaplan matches your style, budget and dates, then builds the itinerary. Ask her for a tailored quote.

When couples ask me for the best honeymoon destinations, my first question is never a place, it is a feeling. Do you want to do nothing at all, or do you want a trip full of moments you will retell for years. That answer sorts most of the choices. Some of the best honeymoon destinations are quiet beaches where the only decision is which lounger to take, and others are once-in-a-lifetime adventures like an African safari or a slow week across Europe. I am Lynne Kaplan, and safaris, honeymoons and luxury travel are the trips I love to plan.

There is no single ranking of the best honeymoon destinations that fits everyone, and any list that claims there is will steer you wrong. What matters is your pace, your budget and the time of year you can travel. From there I shape the options, whether that means all inclusive honeymoon packages where the price is settled before you leave, honeymoon packages that combine a city and a beach, or African safari packages paired with a few nights of pure rest. Once I understand what you two are picturing, I handle the flights, the rooms, the transfers and the little touches that make it feel like a honeymoon and not just a vacation.

Start with what the two of you actually want

The happiest honeymoons come from an honest conversation about how you like to travel. A couple who wants to sleep late and never check a watch needs a very different trip from a couple who wants a stamp in the passport and a story to tell. Many of my favorite honeymoons mix two ideas, a safari then a beach, or a city then a private island, so you get both the adventure and the rest.

What an African safari package actually involves

A safari sounds complicated, and left to chance it can be, which is exactly why couples hand it to an advisor. When I build African safari packages I am coordinating parks, countries, small aircraft, lodges and the seasons, so the trip flows instead of feeling like a series of logistics. Here is what goes into the planning.

Parks and countries

Kenya and Tanzania are the classic first safari, home to the Maasai Mara and the Serengeti and the annual wildebeest migration. South Africa is easy to reach and pairs beautifully with Cape Town and the winelands. Botswana is quieter and more exclusive, built around the Okavango Delta. Each country offers a different feel, and part of my job is matching the destination to what you want out of the experience.

Lodges and camps

Where you sleep shapes the whole trip. Options range from comfortable safari lodges to intimate tented camps where you hear the bush at night, up to private conservancies with your own guide and vehicle. On a honeymoon I lean toward the more private, romantic camps, and I match the standard to your budget rather than assuming the most expensive is the right one.

Best seasons

Timing is everything on safari. The dry months generally bring the best game viewing because animals gather at water and the grass is low. The migration moves through the Mara and Serengeti at predictable times of year. Green season is lush and quieter with fewer travelers. Tell me your dates and I will tell you honestly what you will and will not see.

What drives an African safari cost

Couples always ask about African safari cost, and the honest answer is that it depends on a handful of choices rather than one fixed number. The country, the standard of lodge, how many nights you stay, the season, and whether you fly between camps or drive all move the total. Private guiding and remote camps sit at the top, while a well-chosen lodge in a value season can be far more approachable. I will not quote you a figure on a page and hope it still holds. Tell me what you are picturing and I will build a real, current quote around it.

How luxury and all inclusive honeymoons are built

Luxury does not have to mean the highest price in the room. To me it means the trip is handled, the details are right, and you never have to solve a problem on your honeymoon. All inclusive honeymoon packages are popular for a simple reason, the meals, drinks and most activities are settled before you go, so the week feels generous instead of metered. On the higher end, honeymoon packages can add private transfers, a suite upgrade, a special dinner on the beach and a room ready the way you would want it. I arrange these quietly with the resort so they feel like a gift rather than a line on an invoice.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best honeymoon destinations?

There is no single winner. The best honeymoon destinations for you depend on whether you want a beach, a safari, a European city or a combination, plus your budget and your dates. Lynne Kaplan matches those to the right destination and builds the trip. Ask her for a tailored quote.

How much does an African safari cost?

African safari cost varies with the country, the standard of lodge, the number of nights, the season and whether you fly between camps. We do not quote a fixed figure online because it would quickly be out of date. Share what you are picturing and Lynne will prepare a current, tailored quote.

Can you combine a safari with a beach stay?

Yes, and it is one of the most popular honeymoons we plan. Many couples pair African safari packages with a few nights on a beach or island afterward, so they get the adventure and then the rest. Lynne routes the flights and transfers so the two halves connect smoothly.

What do all inclusive honeymoon packages include?

All inclusive honeymoon packages typically cover your room, meals, drinks and most activities in one price settled before you travel. Specifics vary by resort, and honeymoon extras like upgrades or a private dinner can often be added. Lynne confirms exactly what is included before you book.

When is the best time to go on safari?

It depends on the country and what you want to see. Dry months generally bring the strongest game viewing, and the wildebeest migration moves through the Mara and Serengeti at set times of year. Green season is quieter and lush. Tell Lynne your dates and she will advise honestly.
